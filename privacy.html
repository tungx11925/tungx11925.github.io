Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

1. Introduction

AI Agent Social Manager ("the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

2. Data We Collect

When you connect your TikTok account, we collect and store:

  • Your TikTok Open ID (a unique identifier provided by TikTok)
  • Your TikTok display name and account name
  • Your TikTok access token (stored encrypted using AES-256)
  • Content (text and video) that you create and schedule through the App

We do not collect passwords, personal financial information, or any data beyond what is necessary for the App's functionality.

3. How We Use Your Data

Data collected is used solely to:

  • Authenticate with the TikTok API on your behalf
  • Publish video content to TikTok at your scheduled times
  • Display your account information within the App

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security

All data is stored on your private local server. Access tokens are encrypted using AES-256 encryption before being stored in the database. No data is transmitted to external servers other than TikTok's official API endpoints.

5. TikTok API Data

Data obtained through the TikTok API is used only for the purposes described in Section 3. We comply with TikTok's Platform Policy and do not use API data for any purposes beyond the declared functionality.

6. Data Retention

Your TikTok account credentials are retained until you disconnect the account through the App's settings. Post history is retained for operational analytics and can be deleted upon request.

7. Your Rights

You may disconnect your TikTok account at any time through the App settings. Upon disconnection, your access token is deleted from our database.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Continued use of the App after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
